Plug & Produce and Multiagent

CMAS

CMAS is a framework for Plug & Produce that makes it possible to use configurations rather than programming to model and control a manufacturing system. The key tasks are to define skills on resources, and goals for parts. Goals are central in the design of autonomous multi-agent systems. The final goal for a part is to become manufactured and this can be divided into several subgoals.

Goal-oriented multi-agent control is one approach to handle flexible and reconfigurable manufacturing systems. Goals are important to be able to create autonomous agents. An autonomous agent must have the possibility to decide not only how to achieve a goal, but also which goal to select. For parts that will be manufactured, a state-oriented model is adopted implying that each agent associated with a part tries to reach a final state by executing all assigned subgoals. When all subgoals are finalized, the part is ready, and the implicit main goal is fulfilled. A part is defined as a part of a product or the product itself that will be manufactured within the boundary of the actual manufacturing system. Hence, a subgoal can only be fulfilled by utilizing skills on resources within this boundary. To assist part-agents to reach their subgoals, process plans exist that defines skills needed to reach a specific subgoal. Process plans of subgoals are defined without specifying resources, only required skills, requirements, and abstract interfaces are specified. Abstract interfaces are connected to a resource-agent when needed during runtime, the resource-agent are selected after a negotiation process. Hence, the process plans of subgoals separate the resources from the subgoals. This makes it possible to have several potential solutions in the system for the autonomous agents to select the most suitable process plans. A subgoal depicts the future desired state of the system. However, the sequence of subgoals is important, methods to configure and execute concurrent and parallel sequence of subgoals. Plug & Produce systems, controlled by C-MAS, enables the possibility to rapidly plug in and out resources to adapt to the actual manufacturing situation. Resources prepared with suitable skills and process plans can be plugged in without doing any software reconfiguration of the C-MAS. Parallel subgoals can utilize resources concurrently in parallel. If only one resource is available, subgoals that are configured to execute in parallel will be executed in sequence instead by the single resource. This approach enables the possibility to rapidly avoid bottlenecks in the production that can appear due to for example changed customer orders just by plugging in suitable resources. No reprogramming or software reconfiguration is needed in these situations. A physical Plug & Produce demonstrator was created to evaluate the C-MAS system and the possibility to execute concurrent and parallel subgoals on resources applied on an industrial wooden house wall prefabrication case.
